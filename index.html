Target Decision-Makers Who to reach and why
Commercial Property Owner
The ultimate decision-maker and check-signer. Owns the building directly — deferred roof maintenance is their capital risk. RoofGuard converts that unpredictable cap-ex into a predictable monthly operating expense. Highest close rate when reached directly.
LinkedIn Email Phone Associations
Portfolio Manager
Manages multiple properties under one ownership entity. A single RoofGuard conversation can convert an entire portfolio at once. Motivated by standardization, cost predictability, and reducing emergency maintenance across assets.
LinkedIn Email Phone Associations
Property Manager
Day-to-day operator of the building — the person who gets the call when a roof leaks at 2am. They feel the pain of deferred maintenance most directly. Strong internal champion if they understand RoofGuard removes that liability from their plate.
LinkedIn Email Phone Associations
Asset Manager
Responsible for maximizing property value and managing risk across a portfolio. RoofGuard directly protects asset value and reduces liability exposure — frames perfectly as a risk management and NOI protection decision, not a maintenance contract.
LinkedIn Email Associations
Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
Controls the budget and signs off on operating vs capital expenditure decisions. RoofGuard's core financial pitch — converting unpredictable cap-ex into scheduled op-ex — is a CFO-level conversation. Particularly relevant at mid-size commercial owners without a dedicated facilities VP.
LinkedIn Email Phone
Director of Real Estate
Oversees the real estate portfolio strategy for companies that own or occupy significant commercial space. Has influence over vendor selection and long-term facility agreements. Reached most effectively through commercial real estate associations and LinkedIn.
LinkedIn Email Associations
VP of Facilities
Owns the facilities budget and vendor relationships at the corporate level. Has direct authority over roof maintenance contracts. Typically found at larger companies with 100k+ sq ft of owned or leased commercial space. Best reached via LinkedIn and industry associations.

Services We Offer What we match companies against
Primary Packaging
Direct product contact packaging — bottles, bags, pouches, cans, blister packs, tubes, trays. First layer that touches the product.
Secondary Packaging
Grouping and outer packaging — cases, cartons, shrink wrap, multipacks. Protects primary packages and enables retail display.
Value-Added Packaging
Kitting, co-packing, labeling, custom assembly, gift sets, seasonal bundles, promotional packs, and contract packaging services.
3PL / Fulfillment
Third-party warehousing, pick and pack, order fulfillment, returns management, and inventory control for DTC and wholesale channels.

Target Decision-Makers Who signs or influences packaging and 3PL contracts
VP of Supply Chain / Supply Chain Director
Owns the end-to-end supply chain including packaging sourcing and 3PL vendor selection. The highest-leverage contact — a yes from them moves fast. Most common title at mid-to-large consumer goods manufacturers.
LinkedIn Email Phone Associations
Director / VP of Operations
Responsible for production throughput and operational efficiency. Packaging bottlenecks and 3PL failures land on their desk. Strong champion when you can show speed, capacity, or cost reduction.
LinkedIn Email Phone
Founder / CEO (Small-to-Mid Manufacturer)
At companies under ~$50M revenue the founder often makes vendor decisions directly, especially for packaging and fulfillment. Fastest path to a contract at growing brands and DTC manufacturers scaling into retail.
LinkedIn Email Phone
Procurement Manager / Director of Sourcing
Controls vendor contracts and RFP processes for packaging materials and 3PL services. Gets you into the formal evaluation process. Best path at larger manufacturers with structured procurement departments.
LinkedIn Email Associations
Head of Logistics / Logistics Manager
Day-to-day owner of 3PL and fulfillment relationships. Feels the pain of missed SLAs, warehouse capacity issues, and carrier failures directly. Strong internal champion once they see your provider network across three regions.
LinkedIn Email Phone
Brand Manager / VP of Marketing
Drives packaging design decisions at consumer brands — especially for seasonal, promotional, and value-added packs. Entry point for co-packing and kitting conversations. Relevant at CPG brands where packaging is a brand differentiator.
LinkedIn Email
CFO / VP of Finance
Signs off on 3PL and packaging contracts at smaller companies. Motivated by cost reduction, variable vs fixed cost structures, and outsourcing non-core operations. Strong audience for the financial case for outsourced packaging and fulfillment.
LinkedIn Email Phone
Plant Manager / Manufacturing Manager
Controls production floor decisions including packaging line capacity. Key contact when companies are doing packaging in-house and hitting bottlenecks — strongest signal that outsourced co-packing is a fit.
LinkedIn Email Phone
What to Look For — Buyer Signals Intel that indicates a hot prospect
🔴 Hot Signal
Hiring warehouse, packaging, or logistics staff in-house — signals they're handling it manually and at capacity.
🔴 Hot Signal
Recently raised funding (Series A/B) — scaling product companies that need to outsource logistics fast.
🔴 Hot Signal
Launching new SKUs, seasonal products, or expanding into retail — immediate need for co-packing and kitting.
⚠ Warm Signal
International manufacturer or brand entering the U.S. market — needs domestic packaging and 3PL from day one.
⚠ Warm Signal
Growing DTC brand moving into wholesale or retail — new packaging requirements and fulfillment complexity.
⚠ Warm Signal
Complaints or reviews mentioning shipping delays, packaging quality issues, or fulfillment errors on G2, Amazon, or Reddit.
Key Associations to Target Where packaging and supply chain buyers congregate
PMMI — The Association for Packaging and Processing Technologies
Primary national association for the packaging industry. Members include consumer goods manufacturers actively sourcing packaging solutions.
CSCMP — Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals
VP Supply Chain, Logistics Directors, and Operations leads. National chapters with strong local presence in Ohio, Northeast, and California.
GS1 US — Supply Chain Standards Organization
Members are consumer goods manufacturers requiring compliant packaging and traceability — particularly relevant for retail-bound products.
IWLA — International Warehouse Logistics Association
Warehousing and 3PL decision-makers. Good channel to reach companies evaluating or switching 3PL providers.
Consumer Brands Association (CBA)
Consumer packaged goods (CPG) manufacturers. Members are exactly the buyer profile — brands that manufacture and need packaging + fulfillment.
NAM — National Association of Manufacturers
Broad manufacturer membership across all product categories. Strong for Ag, industrial, and mid-market consumer goods manufacturers.
